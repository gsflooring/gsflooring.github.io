Installation Services

What I Specialise In

From classic hardwood to modern LVT patterns, I bring the same meticulous approach to every installation.

🪵

Hardwood Installation

Solid and engineered hardwood laid with precision. Sanding, finishing, and repairs also available for existing hardwood floors.

🏆

Amtico & Karndean (LVT)

Expert precision-cutting and laying of luxury vinyl tile products. Intricate patterns, borders, and feature designs all undertaken.

💧

Vinyl Installation

Waterproof, durable, and low-maintenance vinyl sheet and plank flooring — ideal for kitchens, bathrooms, and utility rooms.

🧵

Carpet Fitting

Professional carpet fitting across all types: Saxony, Berber, loop pile, and more. Gripper rods, underlay, and edging all included.

📐

Laminate Flooring

Fast, clean, and affordable laminate installation. Ideal for high-traffic areas where durability meets good looks.

🏗️

Screed & Floor Prep

Cement screed levelling and floor preparation to achieve a perfect sub-floor — the critical foundation for a flawless finish.

🔧

Floor Repairs

Squeaky boards, damaged tiles, worn carpet — I diagnose and repair all types of flooring issues to a like-new standard.

📦

Material Sourcing

I can supply all materials through trusted trade accounts, or happily work with materials you've already purchased yourself.
