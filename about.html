Who I Am

Meet Gary Stephenson

I'm Gary — a flooring specialist from Kidderminster, now based in Upton upon Severn. I founded GS Flooring with a single purpose: to deliver exceptional flooring with the care and precision that every home and business deserves.

When I'm not on the tools, you'll find me enjoying live soul music or out on my classic Lambretta scooter — passions that reflect the same values I bring to my work: style, reliability, and lasting quality.

"I started in the flooring trade because I love the transformation — walking into a room once the floor is down and seeing how it completely changes the space. That feeling never gets old."
— Gary Stephenson, Founder
Our Heritage

Rooted in Kidderminster's Carpet Legacy

Kidderminster is renowned worldwide as a carpet-manufacturing hub — a town whose identity is woven from thread, pattern, and an unrelenting pursuit of quality. That heritage is the foundation on which GS Flooring is built.
